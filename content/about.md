# Intro

I am an AI & Machine Learning Engineer and B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Dr. D. Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pimpri, Pune. My work focuses on machine learning, agentic AI workflows, and neural computation from scratch.

I currently work as a Software Development Engineer & Machine Learning Engineer Intern and Project Lead at SprougHub Foundation, contributing to Project JHEP (an AI-powered Learning Management System). Previously, I was a Research Intern at DevKraft contributing to Deepreach, an agentic company discovery and research workflow. I am also the co-inventor of a patent application for proof-based task validation in collaborative environments with Prof. Prajakta P. Pawar.
